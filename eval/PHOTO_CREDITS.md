# Photo credits

Every photo in `eval/photos/` is either an openly licensed image from Wikimedia Commons or a
derivative of one, made for this dataset. No photo was taken from an auction, dealer or stock site.

Five files per equipment unit:

| Suffix | What it is |
|---|---|
| `-whole` | The unit in its room or yard. |
| `-plate` | The nameplate or data plate, straight on. |
| `-plate-angle` | Derived: perspective-warped and rotated copy of `-plate`. |
| `-plate-hard` | Derived: glare, shadow and blur added to `-plate`; the deliberate hard shot. |
| `-tag` | A tight crop of the plate, or of the unit's own painted label, enlarged. |

Author and licence come from each source file's Commons page, which is why the two source
photos for a unit can name different creators. Derived files inherit the licence of the photo
they were made from; where that licence is CC BY-SA, the derived file is distributed under the
same licence.

A few whole shots are cropped from the source to remove bystanders, and a few plates are
cropped to the nameplate; the `Derived from` column says which.
