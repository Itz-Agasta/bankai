# Photo credits

Every photo in `eval/photos/` is either an openly licensed image from Wikimedia Commons or a
derivative of one, made for this dataset. No photo was taken from an auction, dealer or stock site.

Five files per equipment unit:

| Suffix | What it is |
|---|---|
| `-whole` | The unit in its room or yard. |
| `-plate` | The nameplate or data plate, straight on. |
| `-plate-angle` | Derived: perspective-warped and rotated copy of `-plate`. |
| `-plate-hard` | Derived: glare, shadow and blur added to `-plate`; the deliberate hard shot. |
| `-tag` | A tight crop of the plate or of the unit's own label, enlarged. |

Derived files are marked below. They inherit the licence of the photo they were made from.
Where that licence is CC BY-SA, the derived file is distributed under the same licence.

Some units use a cropped version of the source photo to remove bystanders; the crop is noted.
